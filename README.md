## Featured Projects  
### 🔹 [MASTER: Market-Guided Stock Transformer (Reimplementation)](https://github.com/Syed-Murtaza-Ali/Master-Reimplementation)  
From-scratch PyTorch reimplementation of an AAAI 2024 stock-forecasting transformer, benchmarked against the paper's own results:  
- Market-guided gating, intra-stock and inter-stock attention, temporal aggregation  
- Trained and validated across multiple seeds on official CSI300/CSI800 datasets  
- Dedicated leakage tests confirming no information crosses stock or time-step boundaries  

### 🔹 [Public Business Data Pipeline](https://github.com/Syed-Murtaza-Ali/public-business-data-pipeline)  
A re-runnable pipeline collecting, cleaning, de-duplicating and scoring public business contact data:  
- Sourced from OpenStreetMap and Expertise.com with per-source health monitoring  
- Union-find deduplication and 0-100 contact-completeness scoring  
- 7 automated tests covering cleaning, deduplication, and scoring logic  

### 🔹 [Customer Analytics & Strategic Intelligence: HUMNL vs MTL](https://github.com/Syed-Murtaza-Ali/HUMNL-MTL-Customer-Analytics)  
Full-semester customer analytics research comparing two Pakistan Stock Exchange-listed media companies:  
- Financial outlook analysis, KPI estimation, and CLV-based segmentation  
- Persona development, customer journey mapping, and churn-risk modelling  
- Five prioritized strategic recommendations backed by the analysis
